# Getting help

- **Setting up or customising your portfolio:** start with the [README](README.md); [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) covers the architecture and conventions in more depth.
- **Questions and ideas:** open a [discussion](https://github.com/satishjhanwer/react-portfolio-template/discussions) if they're enabled, or an issue otherwise.
- **Something broken:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/satishjhanwer/react-portfolio-template/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Security issues:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

This is a project maintained in spare time, so replies may take a few days.
